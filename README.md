# NBA Stats Hub

A web app for following the NBA: fixtures, results, live scores, box scores, standings, team schedules and player leaders.

It opens on the **2026-27 season**, which is in progress. You can switch to the completed 2025-26 and 2024-25 seasons with the season picker in the header.

## Features

| Page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A dashboard you can arrange (⚙ Customise: show, hide and reorder the cards, kept in your browser): today's games (or the season's last day), your team's next game, the top six in each conference, title odds (or the champion once a season is over), last night's best player, the scoring and awards races, and today's puzzles with your progress. *Last night in brief* lists 40-point games, triple-doubles, new season highs, rookie breakouts and long scoring streaks. |
| **My team** | Your favourite team in one place: countdown to the next game, record and seed, projected wins, playoff/title odds (and No. 1 pick odds if they're in the lottery), last-10 form, the model's pick for each of the next five games, the schedule ahead, leaders, the injury report, this season against last season game by game, and every result. |
| **Scores** | Any day's fixtures, live games and results, with your favourite team's game pinned first and a countdown to its next game. Live games refresh every 30s. *Tonight's best bets* lists the night's likeliest 30-point games, double-doubles and triple-doubles. |
| **Game** | Before tip-off, a preview: the model's pick and margin, both teams' form, leaders and injuries, and this season's meetings. During and after, a live play-by-play feed and 30-second refresh during games (and a "Live now" bar on every page), scoreline, scores by quarter, team stat comparison, a game-flow (lead) chart, win probability, each team's assist network, a shot chart with paint / mid-range / 3PT splits, and full box scores. After the final, *what decided it*: both teams' four factors and which one swung the game. Before tip-off, *by the numbers*: a projected score from both teams' ratings and pace, rest, and the four factors side by side. After the final, a *recap card* (score, quarters, top performers, win-probability line) to save or share as an image. Finished games open on the box score, with tabs for charts, play-by-play and the recap card. Live games open on the *live centre*: win probability and how it has moved in the last five minutes, the current scoring run, team fouls and the bonus, who is on the floor with their lines and foul trouble, and the latest plays. Before tip-off, a *projected box score*: each likely player's minutes, points, rebounds, assists and threes (from his last ten games and the season, adjusted for the opponent, injured players left out) with his chance of 30 points, a double-double and a triple-double. |
| **Player** | A season card (averages and league percentiles) you can save as an image or share, season averages, a per-game trend chart (points, rebounds, assists, threes, minutes) with a 10-game average, passing partners (who they assist and who assists them), a season shot map for high-volume shooters, and the full game log. Also a *season timeline* (one column per team game: points, Game Score, +/- or minutes, with missed games marked) and *shot types* (dunks, layups, floaters, step-backs, pull-ups and more: share, FG% against the league, how many makes were unassisted, and FG% by quarter). An *aging curve* compares their numbers by age with the league average and the five most similar careers, and what those players did next. *This season vs last* compares their numbers and shot diet with last season, with their archetype. *Plays most like* lists this season's players with the closest style (usage, passing, rebounding, defence, shot locations, free throws, self-created shots and shot-type mix). A *season calendar* shows every game as a square shaded by points or Game Score, with missed games marked. The page is split into tabs: *Overview*, *Shooting & passing*, *Game log* and *Career & contract*. |
| **Awards** | A live MVP, Defensive Player of the Year and Rookie of the Year race from a simple, explained model (box-score production, team record, plus-minus and on/off; steals, blocks and team defence for DPOY), with the 65-game rule pro-rated during the season. For 2024-25 the real voting sits alongside: the model had the right MVP and Rookie of the Year. A chart shows how the race moved week by week. While the season is on, *win odds* for each award from simulating the rest of the season; the Rookie of the Year page adds a *rookie ladder* of production against draft slot. *Past winners & voting* has every season's award winners and MVP voting. *All-NBA teams*: the model's All-NBA (three positionless teams of five), All-Defensive and All-Rookie teams. |
| **Projections** | *Playoff odds*: every team's chances to make the playoffs, conference finals, Finals and title, from 2,000 simulations of the rest of the season (incl. play-in and every series), plus how those odds moved week by week. *Power ratings*: Elo ratings through the season, a power ranking, the model's picks for upcoming games, its accuracy and the biggest upsets. *What if*: pick winners for the next 7/14/30 days and watch seeding, play-in and lottery places change. *Injury what-if*: sit any player for the rest of the season or bring back one who is out, and see the team's wins, playoff and title odds change (his Win Shares per game over the games left, as an Elo change). |
| **This week** | A Monday-to-Sunday round-up for any week of the season: team and player of the week, the best performances, players of the week, new season highs, the week's records, standings movers and the biggest upsets (by pre-game Elo). |
| **Standings** | Conference tables with playoff/play-in lines, plus a "games above .500" race chart (highlight up to 3 teams). A *seed by week* view shows each conference's standings over the season. The *NBA Cup* view shows the six group tables (with group winners and wildcards marked, ranked by the Cup tiebreakers) and the knockout rounds. |
| **Playoffs** | The full bracket with seeds, series scores and each series' odds (from Elo with home court). Expand a series to see its games, how the odds moved after every game and the series leaders. Before the playoffs, *if the playoffs started today* shows the first-round pairings and their odds. A *bracket challenge* lets you pick every series before it starts and a champion (kept in your browser, scored as series finish). Before the playoffs, *play-in* odds for every team seeded 7th to 10th (7th seed, 8th seed or out). |
| **Leaders** | Top 50 per-game leaders, a *Season highs* tab with the best single games and most triple-doubles, and *Milestone watch*: active players about to reach a round-number career total or pass someone on an all-time list, with how many games it should take. Also *Advanced* (PER, Win Shares, WS/48, offensive and defensive rating, computed the Basketball-Reference way), *Build your own* (weight nine stats with sliders and the ranking updates live), *On/off* (the league's biggest on/off swings) and *Shot quality* (expected eFG% from shot locations against how much each player beats it). Plus *Win probability added* (who swings games most, from ESPN's win probability on every play) and *Streaks* (active and longest runs of 20-point games, double-doubles, 3+ threes and more). |
| **Rankings** | Every team ranked by any column: record, PPG, points allowed, margin, home/road, close games, best winning run, strength of schedule (played and remaining), shooting %, rebounds, assists, turnovers. Tabs for the *four factors* (shooting, turnovers, rebounding and free throws on offence and defence, shaded by rank), a *luck meter* (actual against Pythagorean expected wins, close games and opponents' three-point and free-throw shooting), *quarter splits* (margin by quarter, half-time leads kept and blown) and *garbage time* (net rating with blowout minutes removed, by Cleaning the Glass's rule, and who scores most in them). Also *SRS & title DNA* (Simple Rating System, strength of schedule, and the past champion each team plays most like), *scoring runs* (10-0 runs made and allowed, the season's biggest) and *rest & travel* (records on back-to-backs and by days of rest, rest advantage, miles flown, time-zone jumps and long road trips). *Team styles*, *points by source* and *schedule ahead* are here too. |
| **Teams** | Record, this season against last season game by game, the injury report, margin-by-game and games-above-.500 charts, a season *who assists whom* network plus passer × scorer grid, a season shot map (frequency and FG% vs league by spot), *lineups & on/off* (most-used five-man units with +/- per 48, and each player's on/off swing, inferred from play-by-play substitutions), schedule as a list or month calendar, a ☆ to make it your favourite, and an *Add to calendar* (.ics) download. Also an *Elo rating* chart (this season against last, biggest swings), a *who plays with whom* heat map (minutes together for the ten most-used players, +/- per 48 on hover) and *where the points come from* (paint, mid-range, threes, free throws and fast breaks against the league). Also *quarter by quarter* margins and lead records. Also a *style profile* against the league with the champions it plays most like, *with and without* records for each regular, and a *lineup explorer* for any two to five players. Plus a *rotation* chart (how often each player is on the floor in every 3-minute stretch), *opponent shooting by zone* with each player on court, and *clutch lineups*. A *season report* link opens a one-page summary (ratings and ranks, four factors, players, lineups, quarters, payroll, the champion it plays like) ready to print or save as a PDF. Team pages are split into tabs: *Overview*, *Lineups & rotation*, *Shooting & passing*, *Schedule* and *Payroll*. Shot maps group shots into bigger areas by default (4 ft squares for teams, 6 ft for players) so each holds enough shots to compare with the league, with a *Finer detail* switch. |
| **Salaries** | Every team's payroll as a bar split into its players' contracts, against the minimum, the cap, the tax line and both aprons (official league figures), each team's status (under the cap, taxpayer, above an apron…), the biggest contracts and the best value for money. Team pages show their payroll, every contract for the next five seasons with options marked, and salary committed by season; player pages show salary by season. A *salary vs production* scatter flags the best and worst value contracts. *Win Shares per $10M* lists the best and worst value contracts. |
| **Trade machine** | Build a two-team trade from both payrolls: whether the salaries match under the cap and apron rules (simplified from the 2023 agreement), each payroll and cap status afterwards, and the Win Shares that change hands. Trades are saved in the address, so they can be shared. Trades can include a third team (choose where each player and pick goes) and each team's own draft picks for the next seven years, and show every team's playoff and title odds after the trade. |
| **Draft hub** | This season's projected draft order and lottery odds (with a button to run the lottery). From the 2027 draft it uses the NBA's new *3-2-1 lottery*: 16 teams and 37 balls, every lottery pick drawn, 3 balls for teams outside the play-in, 2 for the three worst records (relegated, but never below 12th) and for 9th and 10th seeds, and 1 for the losers of the 7-v-8 play-in games; earlier drafts keep the 14-team format, every team's draft class since 1990 graded against what its picks usually produce (with the biggest steals and lottery disappointments), and which franchises have drafted best. It also holds *every draft class* since 1947 (redraft, best pick at each slot, steals, No. 1 picks) and *every lottery* since 1985. A *lottery race* charts each team's No. 1 pick odds week by week, with a relegation watch for the three worst records. The 3-2-1 repeat limits apply: last year's No. 1 can't win it again, and a team that picked in the top five in both of the last two drafts can't again. |
| **Compare** | Up to three players side by side: per-game averages (best highlighted), rolling-average trend lines and their season shot maps. Below the live game logs, a *full report* from the season data: archetype, PER, Win Shares, true shooting, usage, ratings, on/off, win probability added, salary and Win Shares per $10M, shot diet, and points per game by age. A *head-to-head* section shows how the players have done against each other since 2024-25. |
| **H2H** | Pick two teams to see their meetings, head-to-head wins and season numbers side by side. Both teams' season shot maps sit side by side. |
| **Insights** | *Player of the night* (each night's best game by Game Score, plus the season's best). *Hot & cold* (players and teams over their last 5/10/15 games vs the season). *Clutch* (last five minutes, within five points: scoring, shooting and team records). *Duos & trios* (best and worst two- and three-man combinations per 48 minutes). *Shooting zones* (best and most frequent shooters at the rim, mid-range, corner and above-the-break threes). *Schedule ahead* (remaining opponent strength, back-to-backs and road trips from any date). *Most exciting games*: every game ranked by win-probability swings, comebacks, lead changes and late drama, with a scatter of the whole season. *Passing duos*: the league's top passer → scorer pairs and a passing-style chart (ball movement vs one-man show). *Shot types* (leaders by type, self-created scoring and fourth-quarter risers). *Points by source* (every team's scoring split into paint, mid-range, threes and free throws). *Usage vs efficiency* (how much of the offence each player carries against true shooting). *Team styles* (pace against three-point rate, with past team-seasons for context). *Player archetypes* sorts every rotation player into one of eight styles (shot creator, playmaker, slasher, shooter, 3-and-D wing, stretch big, rim-running big, rim protector) and shows each team's mix; the Clutch tab adds *free throws under pressure*. *Hot hand* tests whether players shoot better right after a make, allowing for tougher "heat check" shots. *Shot situations* rates shooting by time into the possession (transition, early, late), second chances, shots after timeouts and the score. (Team styles, points by source and schedule ahead moved to Rankings.) |
| **History** | All-time rosters and stats: a chord diagram of players moving between teams (by era), career journeys with season-by-season stats, honours and career arcs, the journeymen leaderboard, a two-player compare, all-time leaders (totals, per game, best seasons), awards history (MVP voting by season, winners, All-Star selections), *greatest teams* (every team-season ranked by SRS, by era), *similar players* (the closest era-adjusted seasons since 1979-80 to any player-season), *team vs team* (any two franchise seasons side by side, e.g. the 1995-96 Bulls and 2016-17 Warriors, with ratings, shooting and pace against each season's league average, top scorers, and the odds if they met, from each team's SRS), *draft history* (every NBA/BAA draft class, a redraft of any class by career production, the best player taken at each pick, late-pick steals and every No. 1 pick, filterable by team) and a roster-continuity heatmap. Also *Across eras* (career and peak numbers rescaled to any season's pace and scoring), *Hall of Fame odds* (a model trained on who actually got in), *Lottery history* (every lottery since 1985 and the biggest jumps) and *On this date* (a franchise's games on today's date since 2001-02, from ESPN). (The draft and lottery history now live in the Draft hub, and awards history on the Awards page.) |
| **Franchise** | Each franchise's whole history: names and eras, titles, all-time record, a win %-above-.500 bar for every season, franchise leaders (counting only seasons with that team, traded seasons split correctly), its best draft picks and a season-by-season table with SRS, offensive/defensive rating, pace and attendance. Also an all-time starting five (one per position) with a bench. Open it from any team page. |
| **Games** | *Played for both*: a daily 3×3 franchise grid puzzle. *Connect the players*: link two players teammate by teammate. *Guess the player*: name a player from their career path. *Trivia*: five daily questions (a champion, a scoring leader, an MVP, a No. 1 pick, a win total). *Who am I?*: guess the player from one season's stat line. *Higher or lower*: who had more of a career stat? |
| **6 Degrees** | The Kevin Bacon game for the NBA: every player since 1946-47, linked by having been teammates. Pick a centre player to see all 5,400+ players arranged in rings by teammate distance (and around the circle by era), connect any two players by the shortest chain of teammates, and see history's best-connected players. |
| **Fantasy** | Season rankings in ESPN, Yahoo or DraftKings points, or nine-category value; hot pickups (outside the top 100 and averaging 15%+ more over their last five games); and games per team this week and next, with back-to-backs. |
| **Alerts** (🔔) | Since your last visit: your team's results and injury news, and for the players you follow (☆ Follow on any player page) their games, season highs, double- and triple-doubles, streaks and upcoming milestones. Kept in your browser. Optional *game notifications* (while the site is open) when your team tips off, is within five in the last three minutes, and at the final. |
| **Data health** | When each season's data was last refreshed, how complete it is (games, box scores, play-by-play, win probability, injuries, payrolls), the history files, and the daily data job's latest runs. Linked from the guide and search. |
| **Guide** (?) | A tour of every page and a plain-English glossary of the stats (PER, Win Shares, SRS, four factors, WPA, garbage time and more). |

Pages with many views (Insights, History, Leaders, Rankings) group their tabs into sections; on a phone they become one dropdown.

Search (🔍 in the header, or press `/`) finds any team, page or player from any era: current players open their player page, older ones their career timeline.

Your favourite team and season choice are saved in your browser.

The header has a 🔗 share button (copies a link to the page you're on, or opens your phone's share sheet) and a light/dark switch (auto follows your system; light and dark are remembered). The puzzle games also share a spoiler-free result, Wordle-style.

## Data

All data comes from ESPN's public NBA API (no key needed). Parsing is in `src/api/normalize.ts`.

**Completed seasons are bundled with the app.** Loading a whole season live from a browser means about 45 MB of team schedules plus about 100 MB of player game logs for season highs. So the **Season snapshot** GitHub Action (`.github/workflows/snapshot.yml` → `scripts/snapshot.ts`) fetches 2025-26 and 2024-25 once and saves a compact copy in `src/data/season-*.json`. Standings, playoffs, rankings, leaders, season highs, team schedules and H2H for those seasons load instantly, even offline. The snapshot also totals every game's play-by-play into passer → scorer assist pairs, binned shot locations (teams, the league and the top 75 shooters), a "drama" row per game, lineups and on/off, clutch-time totals, two- and three-man combinations, and every player's season box-score totals (which drive the awards race; first-year players are found by matching names against Basketball-Reference). Box scores, player pages and per-day top scorers are still fetched live. To refresh the copies, run the workflow from the **Actions** tab.

The season in progress is snapshotted too, and refreshed daily by a scheduled run of the workflow (GitHub only runs schedules on the default branch, so this starts once the code is on `main`). For that season, standings, single-team schedules and leaders are still fetched live; the snapshot only powers the heavy league-wide views.

**Career stats and awards.** `scripts/careers.ts` builds `src/data/careers.json` (every player's season totals, award voting and All-Star selections, about 1.6 MB) from the same source. The hosted site loads it only on pages that need it.

**Per-game lines.** The snapshot job also writes `src/data/lines-<year>.json`: every player's line in every game (about 1.3 MB per season), loaded only by Player of the night, Hot & cold and the awards timeline.

**Salaries.** The daily snapshot reads each team's payroll page on Basketball-Reference (this season and the next five, with player and team options and non-guaranteed years; one request every few seconds, within their published bot limits) and past salaries from ESPN's roster contracts. Totals are the players under contract, so dead money and cap holds aren't included. Cap thresholds are in `src/lib/salaryCap.ts`.

**Injuries.** The site reads ESPN's league injury report live; the snapshot keeps a copy for when ESPN can't be reached.

**On this day.** `scripts/careers.ts` also writes `src/data/onthisday.json`: for each calendar day, the most notable players born or debuting on it (from Basketball-Reference's career info), shown on the home page with the top scorer from a year ago.

**Franchises and drafts.** `scripts/franchises.ts` builds `src/data/franchises.json` (every team-season's record, playoffs, ratings, four factors and top five scorers, league averages, and every draft pick, about 830 KB). Titles are listed in `src/lib/franchiseData.ts`.

**Loading.** The hosted site loads only what a page needs: the core app is about 95 KB (gzipped), every other page's code loads the first time it's opened, each season's snapshot is fetched on demand, and the history files load only on the pages that use them. The standalone file bundles everything, with the data stored gzip-compressed inside it (about 5 MB instead of 12 MB) and unpacked in the browser when first needed.

**Install it as an app.** On a phone, open the site and choose *Add to Home Screen* (or the *Install app* button on the Guide page in Chrome). It then opens full-screen like an app. The first visit saves the app itself, and every page and dataset you open is kept, so it still works offline with the last data it loaded; a bar at the top says when you're offline.

**Teammate graph.** `scripts/teammates.ts` builds `src/data/teammates.json` (every team-season roster since 1946-47, about 330 KB) from Basketball-Reference data via the open [bball-reference-datasets](https://github.com/sumitrodatta/bball-reference-datasets) project. ESPN has no historical rosters. Until Basketball-Reference adds the season in progress, its rosters come from our ESPN box scores (everyone who has played a game for each team): returning players are matched by name, first-year players are added. The snapshot workflow rebuilds it, with `careers.json` and `franchises.json`, every day.

## Quick start (no install)

Download `standalone/NBA-Stats-Hub.html` and double-click it. It opens in your browser and needs no Node.js or other install. An internet connection is required for the data.

After changing the code, run `npm run build:standalone` to rebuild that file.

## Development

Requires [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (LTS).

```bash
npm install
npm run dev      # http://localhost:5173
npm test         # unit tests for the API normalisers
npm run build    # production build in dist/
```

## Deployment

`.github/workflows/deploy.yml` runs the tests and build on every push and PR. On `main` it publishes `dist/` to GitHub Pages. To turn it on, open **Settings → Pages** and set the source to **GitHub Actions**.

## New seasons

Nothing to do: from September the site and the daily data job move on to the next season by themselves (ESPN numbers seasons by the year they end in, so 2027 means 2026-27). Season dates in `src/lib/seasons.ts` are estimated until you add the real ones to `KNOWN`, and the salary cap figures in `src/lib/salaryCap.ts` are estimated (+7% a year, and labelled as estimates) until you add the league's numbers to `CAP_LEVELS`. Run the snapshot workflow by hand with no input to refresh the current season and the two before it, or give it a list of years.

## Navigation

Home and Scores sit in the top bar with four menus: *This season* (standings, this week, playoffs, projections, awards, draft hub), *Stats* (leaders, team rankings, insights, fantasy), *Teams & players* (teams, my team, compare, head-to-head, salaries, trade machine) and *History & fun* (history, 6 Degrees, games, the guide and data health). Old links to pages that moved redirect to their new home. Pages that always show the current season (alerts, salaries, trade machine) say so when another season is picked.

## Accessibility

The site has a skip-to-content link and visible keyboard focus, labels every control, marks the selected tab or filter for screen readers, keeps text at WCAG AA contrast in light and dark modes, and turns off animation for people who ask their system for reduced motion. Every page passes an automated axe-core check.
